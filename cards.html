Component
Cards
Surface containers for grouping related content — Stat, Appointment, Patient, and List variants.
Purpose

Cards are white surface containers that group related content into a single scannable unit. They provide visual separation from the page background and define a clear boundary around a piece of information or a task. ClearPath FDO uses four card variants: Stat (single metrics), Appointment (full context), Patient (record summary), and List (section with repeating rows).

Surface, not decoration
Cards group related content into a scannable unit. Never use a card purely for visual decoration — every card must contain meaningful, actionable content.
Consistent padding
All cards use 20px or 24px inner padding on the 8px grid. Never mix padding values within the same card grid — visual rhythm breaks immediately.
One primary action
Each card should have at most one primary call-to-action. Secondary actions (Reschedule, Cancel) must be visually subordinate — never competing with the primary.
Never nest cards
Cards inside cards create ambiguous depth. Use list rows, dividers, or subtle background tints to separate sub-content within a single card surface.
